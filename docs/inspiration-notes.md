# Inspiration Notes — Bennion Kearny Tarot Deck (PDF)

Source provided by Helen:
- https://www.bennionkearny.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Tarot-Card-Deck.pdf

## What we’ll use
- General structure inspiration: full 78-card system
- Arcana/suits structure:
  - Major Arcana
  - Wands
  - Cups
  - Swords
  - Pentacles
- Visual pacing reference for card-to-card consistency

## Important licensing note from the PDF
The PDF text states the deck is copyright Bennion Kearny Limited and free for personal use, and not for commercial/paid products or redistribution.

## Project approach
For **Pastel Prophecy**, we will create original artwork and writing inspired by classic tarot structure and symbolism, not copy their card images/text directly.

## Production plan
1. Build original style-consistent prompts for all 78 cards
2. Generate/curate original card art assets
3. Add reveal animations and subtle motion effects
4. Keep your preferred reading format:
   - brief summary per card
   - one combined 3-card summary
   - one shared Do/Don’t list for all three together
